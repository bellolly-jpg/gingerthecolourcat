About Olly Bell

Hello! I'm Olly Bell, a children's book author. I have been passionate about art since I could pick up a pen and love losing myself in experimenting with different media. As a young artist, I find the journey from concept to planning and from creation to finish incredibly satisfying.

I have always loved the art in children's books and the way in which the illustrations bring life to the stories and with my latest project – Ginger the Colour Cat, I have merged my love of art with the idea of writing an inspirational story for young children.

I hope you like it; I really enjoyed the process of writing and illustrating and I know that there will be more to come, so watch this space!
